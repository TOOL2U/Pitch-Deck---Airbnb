Lease Security & Legal Framework

Structural Protection & Compliance
10-Year Registered Leases
All leases exceeding 3 years are registered at the Thai Land Office. This provides legal enforceability and tenant protection under Thai law.
Registration ensures lease terms are binding and transferable, protecting capital investment in property improvements.
Subletting & Commercial Use
Lease agreements explicitly permit subletting and short-term commercial rental operations. This is documented and legally binding.
Subletting rights remove operational ambiguity and protect the business model from landlord disputes.
Power of Attorney (POA)
POA agreements used where required to streamline property management and legal compliance. All contracts drafted bilingually (Thai/English).
Improvement Rights
Tenant has explicit rights to renovate, improve, and remove fixtures at lease end. Capital improvements are legally protected tenant assets.
